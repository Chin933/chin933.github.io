Dataset · Empirical social science

Chinese Cultural Variables

Prefecture- and county-level data on historical Chinese institutions for empirical research: imperial examination degree-holders (Jinshi, Juren, Shengyuan), clan genealogies and ancestral halls, 1936 Protestant missionary presence, and surname concentration (HHI). Pairs deep historical records with modern geographic precision, standardized administrative coding, normalized metrics, and map visualizations.

Method · Python toolkit

temporal-did

Difference-in-differences when the policy shock falls between observed periods — a pervasive problem in historical data. The toolkit diagnoses temporal mismatch, classifies settings into four cases (stable, decaying, decayed effects), and jointly estimates decay rates and treatment effects via weighted least squares, with partial-identification bounds and Monte Carlo validation.

Research tool · Python · MIT

Citation Auditor

Verifies academic citations: whether a cited work exists, whether its bibliographic details are accurate, and whether the source actually supports the claim made about it. Queries five free databases (Crossref, DataCite, PubMed, arXiv, Open Library) with no API keys, returning field-by-field accuracy reports, open-access links, retraction notices, and structured JSON.
