Field Works

Smallholder Credit Survey

Helped design and implement a village-level field survey on smallholder credit access. The survey investigated whether local farmers could obtain credit, how they used it, and whether credit access improved agricultural production and household livelihoods. Local news coverage documented the field investigation.

Media coverage →

Dialect Education RCT

Participated in the field implementation of a randomized controlled trial across public and private schools. Classrooms were randomly assigned to either a dialect education module or an active-control module. We surveyed and tested students' dialect learning.
